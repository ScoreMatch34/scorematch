🚩 100% Corner Kick Goal Formula

  • Target Striker: Place Hammer or Bulldozer in the far-post corner spot.
  • Curve Technique: Draw a high curve line extending slightly outward from corner arc.
  • Execution: Aim directly at Hammer's head. Due to max height and power stats, they overpower defenders every time.

🛡️ How to Stop Fast Strikers (Speedsters/Jets)

  • Rule #1: Never pull your CBs out of position! Let the AI keep your CB line structured.
  • Defensive Lineup: Use fast CBs like Voyager, Raider, or Explorer in central defense.
  • Goalkeeper Choice: Use Stopper for shot savings or Sweeper to intercept deep through balls.

⚡ One-Touch Midfield Passing IQ

  • Chain 1-touch passes using Commander, Guard, and Major in midfield to avoid tackles.
  • Never pass to Speedsters/Infiltrators in tight crowded spaces—only pass into open green grass!
