🚩 100% Corner Kick Scoring Routine

1. Target your Hammer or Commander placed near the far post or central box.

2. Curve the corner ball slightly outward to give maximum elevation.

3. Ensure your target player has high Strength/Height to win the header every time.

🛡️ Defense Rules (How Not to Concede)

• Never select CBs to tackle early! Pull back CDMs or Fullbacks to pressure ball carriers.

• Use Stopper or Sweeper based on your defensive line height.

• Avoid using Speedsters in central defense—always use Guards, Commanders, or Voyagers.
