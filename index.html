Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the best formation in Score! Match?

There is no single “best” formation for everyone, but the most consistent and easiest ones are: 4-4-2 (best for beginners and balanced), 5-3-2 (very strong in Infinity Arena for possession and solid defence), and 3-5-2 (good for midfield control). Other popular options: 4-1-2-3 and 4-3-3.

2. What is the best captain type in Score! Match?

Top choices according to experienced players include Hammer (safest and most reliable), Prowler, Protector, Commander, and Intruder. Avoid making your captain Infiltrator, Architect, or Speedster.

3. How to upgrade players fast in Score! Match?

Play as many matches as possible and open packages regularly, use Package Reward Booster when you have time to play, watch ads to double cards of important players, focus upgrades only on your starting 11, win events, and never waste Bux on unneeded players.

4. How do I unlock Super Players?

Super Players come from Gold, Platinum and Diamond packages (from Arena 3 onwards), events, Season Pass, or occasionally in the Shop for 1500 Gems. The fastest free way is using Package Reward Booster and playing many matches.

5. What is the best formation for Infinity Arena?

5-3-2 is the current meta for most Infinity Arena players because it gives strong defence and good possession. 4-4-2 is also excellent and easier to play.

6. Should I change my captain?

Yes, if your current captain is weak (like Infiltrator or Architect). Change it to Hammer, Prowler, Protector, or Commander. It costs Gems, so wait for a discount if possible.

7. How to defend against volleyball / long balls in Score! Match?

Use fast and responsive centre-backs (Explorer, Intruder, Protector). Don’t let the opponent get free crosses, keep possession so they cannot launch long balls, and use 5 defenders (5-3-2) against heavy volleyball teams.

8. What should I spend Gems and Bux on?

Gems: Package Reward Booster, Super Players from Shop (only good ones), important events. Bux: Player upgrades (only core players), event entry fees. Never waste them on random packages.

9. Which player types are best in defence?

Best centre-backs: Explorer, Intruder, Protector, Commander. Best full-backs: Producer, Protector, Explorer. Avoid slow players in defence at higher arenas.

10. How important is possession in Score! Match?

Extremely important, especially in higher arenas and Infinity Arena. If you keep the ball, the opponent cannot score. Safe short passes are better than risky long balls.

11. Can I borrow captains from friends?

Yes. You can add Facebook friends or use Friend Codes to borrow their captains. Many players do this to get strong Level 10/11 captains early.

12. What is the easiest formation for beginners?

4-4-2 is the easiest and most consistent formation for new players. It teaches good passing patterns and is balanced in attack and defence.
