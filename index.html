🎯 100% Corner Kick Scoring Routine

1. Target Selection

Always place your highest aerial strength players (Hammer, Bulldozer, or Major) in the central penalty box positions during corner setups.

2. Trajectory & Curve

Draw a smooth curved line aiming slightly behind the front defender, dropping the ball directly onto your Hammer or Major's head at maximum height.

3. Far-Post Finishing

When executing the header, tap towards the far post corner. This leaves goalkeepers like Stopper or Sweeper unable to make a save.
